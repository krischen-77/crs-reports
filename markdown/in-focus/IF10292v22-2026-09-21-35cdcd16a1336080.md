Both invoking Treasury’s authority to use “extraordinary
measures” to stay under the debt limit and temporarily
suspending the debt limit can postpone when Congress
must act on debt limit legislation. The authority for using
such extraordinary measures, which include suspensions
and delays of some debt sales and auctions, underinvest-
ment and disinvestment of certain government funds, and
exchange of debt securities for debt not subject to the debt
limit, rests with the Treasury Secretary.

Invocation of extraordinary measures has delayed required
action on the debt limit by periods ranging from a few
weeks to several months. Temporary suspensions delay the
restrictions imposed by the debt limit for a period
determined by corresponding legislation, and have been
used in lieu of increasing the debt limit to a specific dollar
value in recent years. Current law provides a nominal debt
