Pakistan has suffered considerably from domestic terrorism
since 2003, and related fatalities peaked in 2009. Many
observers predicted a resurgence of regional terrorism and
militancy in the wake of the Afghan Taliban’s 2021
takeover, and data show this has occurred: After five
consecutive years of declining fatality rates down to 365 in
2019, the number of terrorism-related deaths in Pakistan is
up every year since, spiking to 4,001 in 2025, the highest
toll in 11 years (see Figure 1). By many accounts, Pakistan
currently is the country most impacted by terrorism.
