November 6, 2026

Conflict is spreading in Ethiopia, where eight opposition groups      Figure 1. Map of Ethiopia
have formed an armed alliance against the government of Prime
Minister Abiy Ahmed, and the country may be heading toward
war with neighboring Eritrea. Ethiopia’s overlapping rebellions
and its mounting tensions—not only with Eritrea but also with
Egypt and Sudan—threaten to spur a broader regional conflict in
which Middle East powers could take sides.

The governments of Ethiopia and Eritrea have traded allegations
for over a year: Eritrea asserts that Ethiopia—landlocked since
Eritrea became independent in 1993—seeks war as a pretext to
forcefully acquire sovereign access to the sea, and Ethiopia has
accused Eritrea (and Egypt) of waging a proxy conflict through
rebel groups. U.S. officials have sought to defuse tensions, but
