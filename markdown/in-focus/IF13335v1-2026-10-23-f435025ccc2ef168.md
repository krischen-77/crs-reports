how various loan-servicing-related issues may have affected
these patterns and borrower credit scores. For purposes of this In
Focus, loan servicing refers to daily management of a loan. The
tasks associated with loan servicing are typically performed by
ED-contracted servicers. Their duties include issuing billing
statements, collecting regular payments, administering
forbearances and alternative repayment plans for past-due
