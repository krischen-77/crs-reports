Past Anniversary Time Capsules

At least two previous milestone anniversaries of the Declaration
of Independence involved time capsules as part of the
celebration—for the 1876 centennial and the 1976 bicentennial.

Centennial (1876)
In 1876, Philadelphia hosted the Centennial Exhibition to
celebrate the 100th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration
of Independence. For the 1876 Centennial Exposition, Mrs.
Anna Deihm, widow of Civil War veteran Charles F. Deihm,
facilitated the creation of a safe to be filled with memorabilia
from the centennial celebrations. In 1879, Deihm offered the
safe as a gift to the United States, with the expectation that it
would be opened in 1976 by the President of the United States.
In February 1879, Congress held a ceremony in the Capitol to
formally close the safe. On January 19, 1976, at the beginning
of the 2nd session of the 94th Congress (1975-1976),
Representatives and Senators attended a ceremony to open the
outer doors of the safe, which was then put on display for public
viewing. Figure 3 shows the Centennial Safe as opened by
President Ford in 1976.
