For plan year 2026, CMS altered the parameters of the
demonstration to reduce the subsidies available to plans.
The direct subsidy applied to plan premiums was lowered
from $15 to $10. CMS also eliminated the narrowed risk
corridors that were available for plans in 2025.
Additionally, CMS chose, for the first time, to reject some
PDP bids that displayed significant year-over-year premium
increases and were outliers in their Part D region. On July
28, 2026, CMS announced that the demonstration would
conclude with the end of the 2026 plan year, and that
analysis of PDP bids suggested that the additional subsidies
were no longer required to stabilize the PDP market.
