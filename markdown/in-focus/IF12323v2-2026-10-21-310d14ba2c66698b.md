average life expectancy.

Retirement Age
The retirement age was 65 at the inception of Social
Security in the 1930s. In 1983, Congress increased the
retirement age as part of the Social Security Amendments
of 1983 (P.L. 98-21), which made major changes to Social
Security’s financing and benefit structure to address the
system’s financial imbalance at the time. Among other
changes, the retirement age increased gradually from 65 to
67 for workers born from 1938 to 1960. Under the
