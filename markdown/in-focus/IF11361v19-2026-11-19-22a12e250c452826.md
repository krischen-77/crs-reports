U.S. Policy and Congress
Congress authorized U.S. membership in the World Bank in
the Bretton Woods Agreements Act (22 U.S.C. §§286 et
seq.). The Department of the Treasury manages day-to-day
U.S. participation in the Bank. Congress shapes U.S. policy
at the World Bank through a variety of tools, including
• authorization and appropriation of U.S. financial
  commitments to the World Bank;
• legislation directing U.S. representatives at the World
  Bank to advocate for specific policies and vote in a
  manner that advances such policies;
• legislation requiring reports from the Administration on
