Overview of the IRS Budget

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is responsible for collecting nearly all of the federal revenue to fund
federal government agencies and programs, as well as for enforcing taxpayer compliance with federal tax
laws through taxpayer assistance and audits. According to FY2025 data, the agency processed nearly
271 million tax returns, collected $5.3 trillion in gross revenue, and issued refunds totaling nearly
$358 billion.

The IRS’s operating budget includes both annual appropriations and other funds. In FY2026,
appropriations accounted for 67% ($11.2 billion) of the budget. The remaining 33% ($5.6 billion) came
from other budgetary resources, primarily from $4.5 billion in multiyear funding provided by P.L. 117-
169, the FY2022 reconciliation law commonly referred to as the Inflation Reduction Act. Smaller
budgetary resources included unobligated balances from previous years, resources from other accounts,
and reimbursable items.

FY2027 Budget Request

The Administration’s FY2027 appropriations request for the IRS is $9.8 billion, a 12% decrease from the
FY2026 enacted amount. Requested funds include an 18% decrease in funding for both enforcement and
technology and operations support activities, along with a 3% increase in funding for taxpayer services.
The FY2027 request also includes $6.1 billion in other funding not provided by appropriations acts,
including $4.2 billion in mandatory P.L. 117-169 funding, $1.3 billion from user fees, and several
miscellaneous funding sources. Figure 1 provides an overview of the proposed FY2027 budget compared
to the enacted FY2026 levels.

Proposed Budget Changes

The IRS FY2027 appropriations request of $9.8 billion would be a $1.4 billion decrease from the FY2026
enacted amount of $11.2 billion. That decrease is driven by the IRS’s workforce restructuring efforts,
including a $778 million decrease to reduce staff by 4,714 full-time equivalent (FTE) positions and a
$681 million reduction in related nonlabor spending (which includes costs such as contractors and
equipment). These decreases are slightly offset by a $94 million and 1,132 FTE increase for customer
service operations. The budget also includes a $23 million increase for the annualization of a FY2026
1.0% pay increase and an equivalent $23 million decrease due to staffing reductions to offset the cost of
the pay increase.

P.L. 117-169 Funds

The FY2027 budget includes $4.2 billion in P.L. 117-169 funds, accounting for 70% of other budgetary
resources. P.L. 117-169 provided the IRS with $79.4 billion in multiyear funding available for obligation
from FY2022 to FY2031. Of this amount, P.L. 117-169 specified $45.6 billion would be devoted to
enforcement, $25.3 billion to operations support, $4.8 billion to business systems modernization (BSM),
$3.2 billion to taxpayer services, $500 million for energy security, and $15 million for a study on the
feasibility of a direct-file system (these funds were available through FY2023).

Approximately $53.5 billion in P.L. 117-169 funds have been rescinded, primarily enforcement funds.

IRS Activities Funded

IRS appropriations are distributed among three accounts: enforcement, taxpayer services, and technology
and operations support.

Enforcement

This account covers the costs associated with collecting taxes owed, legal and litigation support, criminal
investigations, and enforcement of tax laws. The FY2027 appropriations request for enforcement totals
$4.1 billion, the largest of the three accounts. The request includes $3.3 billion for audits and collections
(an 18% decrease from FY2026), $676.2 million for investigations (a 16% decrease), and $126.4 million
for regulatory services (a 19% decrease).

Taxpayer Services

This account pays for prefiling assistance and education, filing and account services, taxpayer advocacy
services, and associated support costs. The FY2027 appropriations request for taxpayer services totals
$3.1 billion, including $2.3 billion for filing and account services (a 4% increase) and $782.0 million for
prefiling taxpayer assistance and education (level-funded).

Technology and Operations Support

This account funds the operation of the IRS’s infrastructure, including research support, headquarters
maintenance, information systems development and operation, and telecommunications. The FY2027
appropriations request for technology and operations support is $2.6 billion, including $1.1 billion for
shared services and support (level-funded), $1.1 billion for information services (level-funded), and
$331 million for infrastructure (a 63% decrease).

Considerations for Congress

As Congress deliberates FY2027 funding for the IRS, it might consider the impacts of the changes in
enforcement funding and the workforce in the appropriations request. The IRS anticipates the workforce
restructuring efforts to “yield significant savings to the taxpayer, once fully implemented.” The
Congressional Budget Office previously estimated that rescissions to enforcement funding would reduce
tax revenue collected. The National Taxpayer Advocate has expressed concern that reduced IRS staffing
would reduce agency services and taxpayer assistance.
