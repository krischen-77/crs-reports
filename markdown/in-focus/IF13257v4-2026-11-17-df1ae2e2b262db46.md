To study short- and long-term changes to the ocean and climate
system, such as AMOC, scientists rely on sustained global in
situ (i.e., in place) ocean observation networks (e.g., buoys,
floats, moorings). The National Oceanic and Atmospheric
Administration (NOAA) and National Science Foundation
(NSF) manage and support many of these global ocean
observations, making up a large portion of the global in situ
ocean observations. The U.S. contribution is outsized compared
to the contribution of other countries, as it deploys ocean
