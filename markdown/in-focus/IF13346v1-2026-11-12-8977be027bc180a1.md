Contribution to Economic Growth
One of the main ways in which AI has impacted the economy is
in its effects on output, as measured by gross domestic product
(GDP). GDP accounts for all expenditures within the economy,
including businesses’ investment in the plant (e.g., data centers),
equipment (e.g., hardware), and intellectual property (e.g.,
software and research) needed to build AI.
